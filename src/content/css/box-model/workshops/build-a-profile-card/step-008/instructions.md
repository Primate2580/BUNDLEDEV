Paragraphs also have default vertical margins. Setting them yourself makes the spacing predictable. `margin: 16px 0` means `16px` above and below, and none at the sides.

Vertical margins between neighbouring blocks **collapse**: where two margins meet, you get the larger one instead of their sum. Remember that when spacing looks smaller than expected.
