The title looks like any other paragraph. Make it the strongest thing on the page: a larger size, a heavier weight and a tighter line height. Big text needs less leading, so a headline of two lines does not look loose.

`2.5rem` is 40px at the default size, `800` is extra bold, and `1.15` is a line height of 46px for that text.

## Task

Add `font-size: 2.5rem;`, `font-weight: 800;` and `line-height: 1.15;` to the `.post-title` rule.
