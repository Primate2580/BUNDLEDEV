The section headings need a level of their own: clearly smaller than the title, and clearly bigger than the body. That gap between sizes is what gives a page a visible structure.

`1.5rem` is 24px, and a line height of `1.3` suits headings that might wrap onto two lines.

## Task

Add `font-size: 1.5rem;` and `line-height: 1.3;` to the `.section-title` rule.
