The footnote is the quietest text on the page: small, and tucked to the right, away from the main reading line. Alignment moves the content inside a block, not the block itself.

`text-align: right` pushes the footnote text against the right edge of the column. For long paragraphs, left alignment is better, but for a short note, it is a fine choice.
