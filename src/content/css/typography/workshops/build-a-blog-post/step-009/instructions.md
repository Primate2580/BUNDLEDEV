The opening paragraph, the lede, introduces the article, so it should stand just above the body text. A step up in size, with a line height a little tighter than the body's, does the job.

`1.375rem` is 22px, and `1.5` gives it 33px lines. Larger text can use slightly less line height than the body, which is `1.6`.

## Task

Add `font-size: 1.375rem;` and `line-height: 1.5;` to the `.lede` rule.

- Both go in `.lede`.
- `font-size: 1.375rem; line-height: 1.5;`
