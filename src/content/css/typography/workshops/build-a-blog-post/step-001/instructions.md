The whole article is plain Times at the moment. Font properties are inherited, so one rule on `body` sets the typeface for everything inside the article.

`Georgia, "Times New Roman", serif` is a font stack: Georgia first, then Times New Roman, then any serif font. The generic `serif` at the end is the safety net, and names with spaces go in quotes.

## Task
