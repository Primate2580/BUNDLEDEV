Last, the problem the fixed banner created. Because it takes no space, the last lines of the article now slide under it when you scroll to the bottom. The fix is to reserve room: give the page as much bottom padding as the banner is tall.

`4rem` is a little more than the banner needs. Reserving the room means the reader can always scroll the final paragraph clear of the banner.
