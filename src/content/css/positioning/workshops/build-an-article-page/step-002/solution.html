Planning a trail trip

Planning

Start by choosing a route that matches your fitness and the time you have. Check the forecast the day before, and again on the morning of the trip.

Tell someone where you are going and when you expect to be back. A short message can make all the difference if plans change.

Gear

Carry water, a map and a spare warm layer, even on short walks. Weather on higher ground changes faster than it does in the valley.

Wear shoes that you have already worn in. A blister on the first day makes every kilometre after it feel longer.

Safety

Turn back early if the weather changes. A shorter day is always better than an emergency on the ridge.

Keep to marked paths to protect the ground and the plants, and carry out everything you carried in.
