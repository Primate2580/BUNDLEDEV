As you scroll, the article may slide over the header rather than under it, because positioned elements are painted above ordinary content in order. A `z-index` makes sure the header is above everything it passes over.

Sticky elements are positioned, so `z-index` works on them. `10` is a modest number from a small scale: bars at 10, popups above that.
