Shadows have colours too. A pure black shadow looks heavy. A shadow tinted with the brand colour looks softer and part of the design. The last value in `box-shadow` is the colour, and here it uses alpha: indigo at 25%.

The first three lengths are the vertical offset, the blur and the spread of the shadow: `0 12px 32px`. A later lesson covers them. For now, focus on the colour at the end.

## Task

Add `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px rgb(79 70 229 / 0.25);` to the `.plan` rule.
