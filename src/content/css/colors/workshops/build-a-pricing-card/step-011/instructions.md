The fine print should be quiet. A text colour can carry alpha too: dark navy at 70% lets the white card blend into it, which gives a softer grey than a solid colour would.

Be careful, though. Alpha on text lowers its contrast. At 70% this still reaches about 6.6:1 on white, but at 40% it would fail.
