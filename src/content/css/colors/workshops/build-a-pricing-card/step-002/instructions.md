The card needs its own surface so it stands out from the page, and a text colour. Because `color` is inherited, setting it on the card colours the plan name, the price, the description and the list inside it.

`#0f172a` is a very dark navy. It is a softer choice than pure black, and it still has excellent contrast on white, about 17.9:1.

## Task

Add `background-color: #ffffff;` and `color: #0f172a;` to the `.plan` rule.

## Hints

- Two declarations in one rule: the fill and the text colour.
- `background-color: #ffffff;` and `color: #0f172a;`
