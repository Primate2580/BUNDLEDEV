The page is plain white, so the card has nothing to stand out against. Colour starts with the biggest flat area on the screen: the page background.

`background-color` takes any colour value. `#f1f5f9` is a hex colour: a `#` followed by red, green and blue pairs. All three pairs are high and close together, which makes a very pale, cool grey.

## Task

Add `background-color: #f1f5f9;` to the existing `body` rule.

## Hints

- The `body` rule is at the top of the CSS.
- `background-color: #f1f5f9;`
