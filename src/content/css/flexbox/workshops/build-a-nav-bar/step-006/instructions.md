Headers often need a consistent height whatever is inside them. With `min-height`, the header is at least that tall but can still grow if its content needs more room.

Because the header has `align-items: center`, the items stay vertically centred as the height changes. That is the benefit of centring with flexbox instead of guessing at padding.

## Task

Add `min-height: 64px;` to `.site-header`.
