The three items now sit in a row, bunched at the left. That leftover space on the right is **free space** on the main axis, and `justify-content` decides where it goes.

`space-between` pushes the first item to the start, the last item to the end, and shares the leftover space evenly between them. With three items, the middle one lands in the centre.
