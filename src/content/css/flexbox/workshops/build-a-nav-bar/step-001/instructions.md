Right now the three parts of this header (the logo, the links and the button) stack or flow one after another. To lay them out side by side, you turn the header into a **flex container**.

That one declaration makes the header's direct children flex items: the logo, the `nav` and the button all become items in a row.

## Task

Add `display: flex;` to the `.site-header` rule.

## Hints

- The `.site-header` rule is the one with `padding` and `background`.
- Add the line `display: flex;` inside it.
