The footer needs a divider above it. A border on one side only is perfect: `border-top`. Here it is written as three longhands, with a dashed style to show the difference from the solid frame.

Notice that a border on a single side still needs all three parts. Without a style, nothing would appear.

## Task

Add `border-top-width: 1px;`, `border-top-style: dashed;` and `border-top-color: #cbd5e1;` to the `.footer` rule.
