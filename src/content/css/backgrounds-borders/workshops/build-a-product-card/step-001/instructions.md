The card is a white rectangle on a pale page, so its edge is hard to see. Draw a thin frame around it. A border needs all three parts: a width, a style and a colour, and the style is the one people forget, since it defaults to `none`.

A 1px solid line in a light grey-blue, `#cbd5e1`, is a quiet way to separate a card from its background.

## Task

Add `border-width: 1px;`, `border-style: solid;` and `border-color: #cbd5e1;` to the `.product` rule.

## Hints

- Three declarations in `.product`.
- `border-width: 1px; border-style: solid; border-color: #cbd5e1;`
