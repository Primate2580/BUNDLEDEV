For a choice from a short list, use a `select`. Each choice is an `option`, with a `value` that is sent, and the text that the visitor reads:

```html
<select id="walk" name="walk">
  <option value="mill-creek">Mill Creek</option>
</select>
```

## Task

Replace the two lines `Which walk?` and `Mill Creek, Ridge Trail, Heron Lake` with a labelled `select` of three options. Use the values `mill-creek`, `ridge-trail` and `heron-lake`.

## Hints

- The label says `Which walk?` and points at the `select`.
- Three `option` elements, with the walk names as their text.
