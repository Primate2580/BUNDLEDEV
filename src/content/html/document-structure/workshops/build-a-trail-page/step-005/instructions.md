The `title` element sets the text shown in the browser tab, in bookmarks, and as the headline in search results. It lives in the head, and every page should have a unique one.

A good title describes the page, and puts the most important words first.

## Task

Add a `<title>` to the head and set it to `Trail Guide`.

## Hints

- Add `<title>` and `</title>` inside the head, with the text between them.
- The whole element is `<title>Trail Guide</title>`.
