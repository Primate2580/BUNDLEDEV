Mill Creek day out

What to bring

  • Boots
  • Water
  • Snacks
    • Fruit
    • Nuts
  • Rain jacket

The route

  1. Park at the old mill
  2. Follow the creek path
  3. Cross the wooden bridge

Back at the car park

  1. Change your boots
  2. Sign the visitor book

Walk glossary

Switchback
A sharp turn that helps a path climb.
Cairn
A pile of stones that marks the way.
Waymark
A painted sign on a post or rock.
It shows which route to follow.
