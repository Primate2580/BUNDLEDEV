A term can have more than one description. A `waymark` is a painted sign, and it also has a purpose. Both descriptions follow the one term:

```html
<dt>Waymark</dt>
<dd>A painted sign on a post or rock.</dd>
<dd>It shows which route to follow.</dd>
```

## Task

Add a third term, `Waymark`, after `Cairn`, with the two descriptions shown above.
