Two lines contain characters that need care. A plain ampersand in `Boots & poles` works in most browsers, but the correct way to write one is the entity `&amp;`. The copyright line needs a copyright sign, and the entity for that is `&copy;`.

```html
Boots &amp; poles
&copy; 2026 Ridge &amp; Valley Park
```

## Task

Wrap the shop line, beginning `Boots & poles`, in a `p`, and change its ampersand to `&amp;`. Then wrap the last line in a `p`, replace the word `Copyright` with `&copy;`, and change its ampersand to `&amp;`.

## Hints
