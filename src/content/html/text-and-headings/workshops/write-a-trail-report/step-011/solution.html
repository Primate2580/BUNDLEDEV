Ridge Trail report

Posted on 9 November 2026 by the park team

The Ridge Trail is open again after two weeks of repairs. Most of the path is dry, and the new bridge over Mill Creek is finished.

Parking is free, and the car park covers 2,500 m2 beside the old mill.

Trail conditions

The lower half is firm and easy. The ridge itself is windy, so bring layers and a cap.

Safety

The ridge path closes at dusk. Do not walk it after dark. If you need help, the SAR team can be reached at the visitor centre.

Boots & poles are on sale at the shop.

Ridge Trail Visitor Centre
14 Mill Lane, Hillford
