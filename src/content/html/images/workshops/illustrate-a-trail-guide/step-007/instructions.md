Now the gallery. These pictures are far down the page, so there is no need to load them until a visitor scrolls near. `loading="lazy"` does that, and the dimensions keep the space reserved:

```html
<img src="images/gallery-1.jpg" alt="..." width="400" height="300" loading="lazy">
```
