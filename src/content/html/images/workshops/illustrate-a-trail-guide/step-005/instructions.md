The bridge picture deserves a caption, so every reader learns when it was finished. A `figure` wraps the picture and a `figcaption`. The caption adds new information, and does not repeat the alt:

```html
<figure>
  <img ...>
  <figcaption>The new bridge over Mill Creek, finished in November.</figcaption>
</figure>
```

## Task

Wrap the bridge picture in a `figure`, and add a `figcaption` after it with a caption of your own.

## Hints

- The `img` goes inside the `figure`, with the `figcaption` below it.
- Keep the caption different from the alt.
