  5. npm run test:content

THE TOPIC (22 lessons)
  11 learn   what a stacking context is, the painting order inside a context, the exact z-index
             rules, everything that creates a context, the trapped z-index, isolation: isolate,
             side effects (containing blocks and clipping), the top layer (dialog and popover),
             managing z-index at scale, stacking with animations, debugging stacking
  2 visualizers (context trap explorer, paint order explorer), 2 predict, 2 lab, 2 debug,
  1 workshop (12 steps: layer a dashboard), 1 quiz (17 questions), 1 review

DESIGN NOTES
  Every auto-checked case reads declared values (z-index, position, isolation, opacity,
  transform, will-change, pointer-events). The preview cannot scroll or overlap on demand, so
  the lessons ask you to look at the preview, and the checks verify the CSS that causes the result.
  No check relies on specificity, !important, or a longhand written after a shorthand.
